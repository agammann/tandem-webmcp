# tandem demo script — target 2:40

## 0:00–0:18 — The problem

On screen: tandem landing and listening workspace.

Narration: “Sound can feel muddy, harsh, thin, or tiring—but most of us do not think in equalizer curves. tandem turns those feelings into controlled listening experiments. Your ears are the eval.”

## 0:18–0:34 — Bring your own agent

On screen: privacy statement, WebMCP status, and suggested prompt.

Narration: “There is no embedded chatbot and no API key. You bring a WebMCP-capable browser agent. Audio stays on this device and is never uploaded.”

## 0:34–0:55 — Strongest WebMCP proof

On screen: show the four registered tools, call `skill_calibrate_listening`, then `get_calibration_state`.

Narration: “The page registers four real tools with `document.modelContext.registerTool`. Two teach and inspect. Two can stage experiments. Notice what is missing: the agent has no tool to listen, vote, approve, save, or export.”

## 0:55–1:18 — Agent stages, human listens

On screen: call `stage_ab_trial`; visible workspace changes; unlock audio; switch A and B; vote A and choose ‘Clearer.’

Narration: “The agent stages two bounded profiles. tandem validates them, randomizes A and B, and hides the settings. Both paths share one synchronized source. I listen, switch instantly, and record the subjective evidence myself.”

## 1:18–1:39 — Adapt to feedback

On screen: reveal trial one; call state; stage trial two; vote or choose no preference.

Narration: “Only after my vote does tandem reveal the mapping and rationale. The agent reads that feedback, changes just one or two meaningful variables, and stages a narrower second comparison.”

## 1:39–2:06 — Final proposal, human approval

On screen: call `stage_final_profile`; compare Proposal and Original; approve visibly.

Narration: “Two completed trials allow a final proposal. The agent explains how it used my actual feedback, but it still cannot approve. I compare the proposal with the untouched original and make the final decision.”

## 2:06–2:28 — Provenance, privacy, export

On screen: timeline, saved profile, JSON export, privacy copy.

Narration: “The timeline makes responsibility legible: agent staged, human preferred, agent proposed, human approved. I can save locally or export the evidence. No audio, path, account, or tracking data leaves the browser.”

## 2:28–2:40 — Close

On screen: return to tagline and A/B controls.

Narration: “The agent designs the experiment. tandem keeps it honest. I provide the judgment only I can make. tandem. Your ears are the eval.”
