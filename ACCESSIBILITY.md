# Accessibility

tandem is designed for keyboard, screen-reader, touch, and low-motion use.

- Semantic headings, sections, buttons, labels, lists, outputs, and status regions
- Visible high-contrast focus rings on interactive elements
- Large A/B and preference controls that do not rely on color alone
- Text labels for WebMCP availability, session status, provenance, EQ bands, and audio paths
- Screen-reader labels for the local file input, spectrum visualization, A/B switcher, final comparison, and feedback controls
- A polite live region for workflow announcements and an alert region for audio errors
- Near-black and warm off-white palette with an accent used as reinforcement rather than the sole signal
- Responsive layout and large touch targets
- `prefers-reduced-motion` support that removes nonessential transitions and smooth scrolling

The audio itself is the subject of the experiment, but every workflow action and state remains readable without depending on hearing. tandem does not make medical or hearing-health claims.
